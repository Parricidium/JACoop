The Jedi Academy single-player campaign in co-op, for 2 to 16 players.
Built on the open-source OpenJK engine. English & French.

📥 Download the latest release (GitHub)  |  Source code  |  ❤️ Support me on Ko-fi

Features

  • The whole campaign in co-op: host or join (LAN or Internet). Each player has their own character, lightsaber, Force powers and progress, all kept in the host's saves.
  • Downed & revive: a fatal blow puts you down, and a teammate holds G to bring you back. Falling into a pit no longer reloads the game for everyone.
  • Mission map with a vote on the next mission, a loadout screen, and cutscenes and videos shown to everyone.
  • Any character or skin, including mod skins (MP or SP), with a 3D preview, changed live even in game.
  • Lightsabers: single, staff or dual, any installed hilt (saber packs included), free RGB blade colour.
  • Mods travel by themselves: skin and saber .pk3 files are shared through the lobby, in both directions.
  • NPC spawner for the host, with a 3D preview.
  • Widescreen & ultrawide (Hor+ field of view), with a new HUD in the style of Jedi: Fallen Order.
  • Optional ray tracing on any OpenGL 4.3 card, no RTX needed: soft shadows, sabers lighting the scene in their colour, reflections, water, lava, texture relief.
  • Ragdoll corpses when pushed or pulled with the Force, optionally synced between players.
  • English & French: each player reads the messages in their own language.

Install

  1. Unzip JACoop-<version>.zip anywhere. It is a standalone folder, and nothing is written into your game's folder.
  2. Start JACoop.exe. It finds Jedi Academy by itself (Steam or GOG).
  3. Main menu > CO-OP > HOST or JOIN.

The host listens on UDP 29070. Over the Internet, forward that port on the host's router, or use a VPN such as Hamachi, ZeroTier or Radmin.
Everyone needs the same version. You need your own copy of Jedi Academy (Steam or GOG, PC).

Known issues

  • A guest sometimes loses their lightsaber after a Force push or pull while holding another weapon. If it happens, please send the qconsole.log of both players (in JACoop\base).
  • A guest can get stuck in guard stance until someone hits them.
  • A guest's saber blade once became short while they were in the lightsaber menu.
  • A guest who joins during a level's opening cutscene can freeze it for the host: join once it's over.
  • Ambient occlusion is heavy on some graphics cards at high resolution.
  • A level 3 Force pull on a teammate throws them quite far.
  • The disruptor scope ring stays an ellipse on wide screens (cosmetic).
  • Only the host can save and load.
  • No kick and no password for the lobby.
